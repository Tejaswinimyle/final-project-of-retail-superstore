# Retail Business Performance & Profitability Analysis

## Project Overview
This project analyses retail sales data to identify business performance, profitability, customer purchasing behaviour, and sales trends. The analysis helps businesses make data-driven decisions related to inventory, pricing, and regional sales performance.

## Objectives
- Clean and preprocess the retail dataset.
- Perform Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA).
- Identify top-performing products and categories.
- Analyse sales and profit across different regions.
- Study the relationship between discount and profit.
- Build an interactive Tableau dashboard.

## Business Insights
- Technology is one of the strongest-performing categories.
- Sales differ across regions.
- High discounts can reduce profitability.
- A small number of products generate a significant share of sales.
- Seasonal sales patterns can support inventory planning.
